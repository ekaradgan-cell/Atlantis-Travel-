Fair Centers We Serve

Beylikdüzü Fair Center

We offer economical and luxury segment hotel options close to Beylikdüzü Fair Center, the largest fair center on the European side.

  • Hotels 5-15 minutes away from the fair area
  • Economical, mid-range and luxury segment options
  • Daily fair transfer service
  • Special discounts for group reservations

Istanbul Fair Center (IFM)

We provide accommodation solutions in strategic locations close to the airport and fair area for Istanbul Fair Center located in Yeşilköy.

  • 10-20 minutes away from the airport
  • Hotels within walking distance to the fair area
  • 24/7 airport transfer service
  • Multilingual support team
