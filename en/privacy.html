Privacy Policy and Personal Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

We process your personal data only to respond to your request.

Data Controller

In accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the data controller of your personal data is Atlantis Travel. Our contact information: info@atlantistraveltr.com

Personal Data Collected and Collection Method

The following personal data is collected through the contact and quote forms provided on our website:

  • Full Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Number of guests and accommodation dates (in quote forms)

The forms may also collect your company name, the service requested, the number of rooms and your message. We do not ask for ID numbers, passport details or health data; please do not share them.

Purpose of Processing Personal Data

Your collected personal data is processed only for the purpose of preparing special accommodation and travel offers for you, providing the most suitable solutions to your request, and communicating with you. Your data is not used for marketing, advertising, or other purposes.

Legal Basis

We process this data on the basis of contract formation and legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f).

Your Rights Under the KVKK

In accordance with Article 11 of Law No. 6698, you have the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • To request information if your personal data has been processed
  • To learn the purpose of processing personal data and whether they are used in accordance with their purpose
  • To know the third parties to whom personal data is transferred domestically or abroad
  • To request correction of personal data if it is incomplete or incorrectly processed
  • To request deletion or destruction of personal data within the framework of the conditions stipulated in the KVKK
  • To request notification of correction, deletion, or destruction requests to third parties to whom personal data has been transferred
  • To object to the emergence of a result against you through the analysis of processed data exclusively by automated systems

Sharing of Personal Data

Your personal data is not sold to third parties. However, when necessary to provide you with service (for example, for hotel reservations or transfer organization), it may be shared with contracted hotels and transfer companies. This sharing is done only for the purpose of fulfilling the service and with necessary security measures taken.

Storage

Form data is not stored in a website database; it is sent only to our company email address. Correspondence is kept as long as the request requires and as required by law.

Cookies

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser. For visitor statistics, Google Analytics runs in Google's Consent Mode. If you do not select "Accept" in the cookie notice, no analytics cookies are stored on your device; only anonymous, cookieless measurement signals that do not identify you (such as page views) are sent to Google. If you select "Accept", Google Analytics cookies are used for visitor statistics. You can change your choice via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS; forms are protected against abuse and your IP address is not stored (only a temporary, irreversible hash is used for rate limiting).

Contact

To exercise your rights under the KVKK or for questions about your personal data, you can contact us:

Email: info@atlantistraveltr.com
