Privacy and Cookie Policy

We process your personal data only to respond to your request.

Data controller

This notice is provided by Atlantis Travel under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Contact: info@atlantistraveltr.com.

Data we process

The details you enter in our quote, trade fair and contact forms: name, company, email, phone, service requested, dates, number of guests and rooms, and your message. We do not ask for ID numbers, passport details or health data; please do not share them.

Purpose and legal basis

We process this data to respond to your request, prepare a quote and handle bookings, on the basis of contract formation and legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f).

Storage and sharing

Form data is not stored in a website database; it is sent only to our company email address. Correspondence is kept as long as the request requires and as required by law. Booking details are shared only with the relevant hotels and transfer providers.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to info@atlantistraveltr.com.

Cookies

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser. Google Analytics is loaded only if you select "Accept" in the cookie notice. You can change your choice via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS; forms are protected against abuse and your IP address is not stored (only a temporary, irreversible hash is used for rate limiting).
