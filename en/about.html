Atlantis Travel

Our Story

Atlantis Travel was founded in 2025 in Esenyurt, Istanbul, by a team with more than 20 years of experience in the tourism industry. Over the years our team has worked with many corporate hotels and with domestic and international institutions and organisations.

Today we run accommodation and transfer operations for private hospital and clinic groups, medical tourism companies, trade fair exhibitors and businesses. With more than 200 partner hotels across Istanbul we offer options for every budget and follow every stay 24/7. Our office near TÜYAP Fair and Congress Center lets us manage team accommodation on site during trade fairs. Every client gets a single point of contact, and pricing, payment and cancellation terms are set individually for each organisation.
